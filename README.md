# Linear Algebra for Data Science

This repository contains structured learning material on **Linear Algebra**, designed specifically for **Data Science, Machine Learning, and Business Analytics** learners.

The focus is on building **conceptual intuition** and connecting mathematical foundations to **real-world data problems**.

---

## 📘 Contents

- **Linear Algebra.pptx**  
  Concept-driven slides covering vectors, matrices, norms, similarity measures, eigenvalues, eigenvectors, and PCA with real-life business examples.

- **6.01_Linear_Algebra_updated.ipynb**  
  Jupyter notebook with explanations, examples, and calculations to reinforce concepts through hands-on learning.

---

## 🎯 Topics Covered

- Scalars, Vectors, and Matrices  
- Matrix Operations and Dot Product  
- Norms (L1, L2) and Distance Measures  
- Cosine Similarity  
- Vector Projection  
- Normalization and Scaling  
- Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors  
- Principal Component Analysis (PCA)  
- Applications in Marketing, Finance, HR, Sports, and Recommendation Systems

---

## 👥 Who is this for?

- Business Analytics students  
- Professionals revising mathematical foundations  
- Educators looking for intuitive teaching material

Linear Algebra is treated not as abstract mathematics, but as the **geometry of data** that powers modern AI systems.
